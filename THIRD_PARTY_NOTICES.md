# Third-party provenance

At initialization this repository contains original planning documents and source
references. It includes **no copied third-party implementation, bundled dependency,
provider executable, logo, screenshot, or product artwork**.

DeepSeek Harness is a candidate foundation, not an installed dependency. Referencing
a project does not incorporate its code or establish endorsement.

Before any third-party material is incorporated, record its project, exact revision,
source path, destination path, license, copyright notices, modifications, and
distribution obligations. Preserve the complete applicable license and notices in
the distributed product as well as the source repository. See
[the licensing decision](docs/licensing.md).

Do not copy an upstream project's own approval of a dependency and treat it as
authorization granted to this project.

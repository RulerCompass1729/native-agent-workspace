## A project manager for each collaboration space

The one queue secretary for all of NAW is replaced by a project manager for each space: one session per space, one space per session, designated by the person only. It adds to its own space's queue, is shown that space only, and receives agents' messages from its own space's members and from sessions in no space. A new space arranges one by default; the person can leave it out. The former secretary becomes the manager of the one space it is in, once, or is only retired. Behaviour and limits are in `docs/space-project-manager.md`. Who may do what and how the secretary retires are a reading of the person's words, not their decision; a space that an agent creates through a background operation gets no manager (product row V25).

Measured with an isolated Claude Code 2.1.332 and a local fake endpoint, nothing sent to the provider: with an API key no model request is made before the first input (60 idle seconds); with a subscription login one request of `max_tokens` 1 with the text `quota` is made a second or two after start, and no second one in four idle minutes. The earlier statement that creating a session calls no model does not hold for subscription logins, and the dialog says so.

Independent review (a separate read-only Claude session) of the first commit required changes; its findings were confirmed in the source before they were repaired:

- An agent in another space could still reach a manager through `workspace_control`: `send`, `queue`, `steer`, `input` and the shared `forward`, `queueShared`, `steerShared` and `run`. They now follow the rule of `workspace_message`. What the person confirmed passes.
- During a rotation a manager's own item could go to its successor before the successor held the role, so an agent could start itself again. The session an unfinished rotation continues from now counts, at the plan and at the send.
- A role record that could not be read widened what a manager was shown. It now limits a session in a space to its own spaces; a merge preview is refused; a manager whose own row is missing is shown no space.
- Setting one manager over an unreadable record dropped the others silently. The record is read once more, and then the person is asked whether to start over.
- A save that failed closed the role everywhere; a secretary that was only stopped when first seen was retired for good; a timeline note that could not be written reported a designation as failed.

The second round passed with two suggestions, both taken: a space the person set or cleared while the former secretary was away is not handed to it later, and the unreadable file keeps its place until the new record replaces it. The third round passed with two notes, left as they are because both fail closed: starting over needs a hard link, so a record path that is a directory or a link to a missing file cannot be reset in the app; and the rotation log is read although only empty places are recorded.

Checks on the final code: the whole suite in seven runs, 10,593 tests, 0 failures, 0 skipped, temporary guard passed in each; 91 guards removed one at a time, each caught by a test. Dialog images in Chinese and English, light and dark, at two widths: one checkbox line, no overflow. No native provider or model was called by the tests.

Not verified: a full real rotation of a manager, the real app's dialogs and menus (the Electron scripts were adapted and only parsed), and an older backend, which neither refuses cross-space messages nor narrows the session list until its sessions move.

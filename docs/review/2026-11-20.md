

## Phone foreground read recovery

The phone now refreshes foreground reads and reconnects after network loss without replaying input. Healthy reads wait five seconds; failures back off from one to thirty seconds, with bounded GET requests. Hidden pages and rejected authority stop retries. Drafts, input focus and uncertain receipts remain scoped to their original device and conversation.

An authenticated session GET may replace a closed app-local reader after rechecking the same issuer, generation, device, actor, origin and canonical scope. Old read/send contexts fail closed; sensitive consent and push stop on source disconnect. No input, answer or decision is automatically submitted. A fixed 503 denotes a transient read source; writes retain their existing unconfirmed behavior.

The existing phone banner consumes fixed health codes through the existing reminder projection. Missing or invalid producer data stays unverified. The wake producer capability is checked explicitly; this change does not create a detector, push subscription or power policy.

Validation: isolated synthetic mobile/related tests 1175 passed, zero failures or skips, including the service runtime closure check. Red/green browser contact checks and raw evidence remain private. Physical network, biometric and lock-screen push behavior are separate acceptance scopes. Independent review remains required before integration.


## Revoke phone access when the local source is disconnected

Phone logout now validates the original authority, origin and session-bound CSRF without requiring an online backend, then runs the existing durable revocation/cleanup path. Other write paths retain their live-source requirement. The HTTP handler already expires the cookie after logout; it is unchanged.

Five regressions fail before the repair and pass afterward: read-only/full access logout while disconnected, invalid credentials/CSRF, logout during a pending reconnect, and the real broker/HTTP handler cookie and reconnect boundary. Focused result: 60/5 before, 65/0 after, with no skips and owned TEMP 0→0. Pairing lifetime and push behavior are unchanged by this increment. Independent incremental review remains required.

## Pairing replacement retirement barrier

The review found that an asynchronous persistence failure could report a phone replacement complete while its previous durable authority remained valid. Replacements now require confirmed retirement of that authority before publishing a new device. A refused retirement keeps the existing phone and rejects the candidate; a confirmed retirement permits the established process-only fallback. Fresh-process pairing also refuses an unknown previous authority. Process restart/profile-replay, exact acknowledgement, changed-authority and concurrent-pause regressions use fake storage only. Native Keychain compatibility remains unverified; independent security re-review is required before integration.

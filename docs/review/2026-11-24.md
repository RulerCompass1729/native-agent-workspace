
## Put the merged confirmation at the start of its group

The desktop draws its merged confirmation on the first card and retains that anchor while any batch member is queued or running. An actionable first card whose submission failed becomes separately available after the other work settles. Grouping, decision order and per-item receipts are unchanged. The phone still uses its existing grouping projection.

A hidden Electron session presented five archive requests interleaved with a separate create request. The merged card moved from the fifth row to the first and stayed at the same position after its first archive completed. All five archive decisions had separate complete receipts, and the create request still awaited confirmation. The related suite passed 47 checks. There were zero product model calls.

Independent review found two variants of an anchor disappearing while a later decision ran, including a lost response to an accepted decision. The regression tests reproduce both. The same reviewer accepted the final fix with zero blockers.

## DSH TUI r4: the log drops sandbox reports, so the witness keeps a heartbeat (Claude 7, 2026-11-18)

Follows "DSH TUI r3: canary exemption, canary SIGTERM, atomic home files" (2026-11-17), whose second sandbox-check run lost the reports of three fixtures. Fixed in 2579a2ac. Synthetic data only, no real key, no person's DSH home, no model, no network beyond loopback except the approved runtime install from the public npm registry.

Cause, measured on this machine. The unified log drops the kernel's sandbox reports from programs that are not Apple's own: Homebrew Node.js always, Homebrew Python at times; curl and perl were never affected. The drops come in system-wide gaps of about 0.6 to 2 s, roughly every 60.1 s. Nothing marks a gap: there is no loss event in the stream, `log show` does not have the reports either, and the kernel still refuses the connection (EPERM). It is not the settle time, the reader's start or its filter: two readers started 30 s apart missed the same beats, and a burst of 193 reports within 50 ms all arrived. Over 20 rounds of the six reported fixtures, 16 of 120 reports never arrived (13.3%). Before r4 the witness confirmed one canary report at launch and then trusted the log, so a refusal inside a gap left no record and did not stop the session. That breaks the r1 B2 requirement (every refusal recorded, the session stopped).

Fix. A canary now runs beside the session for its whole life, under the session's profile and Node.js. The witness writes the next port number (1 to 999) to it every 250 ms, and it makes one refused loopback connection to that port, whose report names the beat. The witness arms on the first beat's report; beats sent before it only delay arming. After arming, a beat whose report has not arrived 1000 ms after it was sent loses the witness (`witness-silent`), as does the canary exiting or refusing input; the session stops with the existing `dshWitnessLost` notice. The log reader ending stays `witness-lost`. Reports arrived within 8 ms in 631 measured beats. Only the running canary's own beat reports are exempt from `onDenial`; any other report from its PID is a refusal. close() ends the reader and the canary with SIGTERM only. No new interface text.

- `scripts/dsh-tui-sandbox-check.cjs` now keeps its own heartbeat under a minimal profile. A fixture during which one of its beats went unseen proves nothing either way, so it runs again, at most three times; a check no attempt cleared fails. A session the app's witness stopped at a missing beat also runs again. New: the normal session must show at least 4 beats; an idle "hold" session must stop by itself at a real drop, which the check's own reader must show as a gap of at least 450 ms in the canary's reports in the 4 s before the stop; and no canary may remain.

# Eight automatic messages an item

The person asked on 2026-11-25 that automatic messages between agents be allowed up to eight for each item. NAW refused the fourth from one sender to one recipient until the person typed in the recipient or, for the butler, the member's own message to it was delivered.

- The limit for each sender and recipient is eight in place of three. Both refusal texts say so in both languages. The count, the minute between messages, the person's reset and the butler's reset by a delivered answer stay.
- When NAW records an item the butler queued as done, the dispatcher tells the backend of the member that had it, once and after the record is saved. That backend checks the exact native session, reads who the butler is now, clears the butler's run to that member and saves; it takes the clear back if it cannot save. Every other sender's run and the member's own run to the butler stay.
- Nothing is cleared for an item that is waiting, failed, cancelled, marked handled, answered from its card, or queued by anyone other than the butler. A backend without the method keeps its limit: after an update, one still running the older version keeps three.

The clear is the one of the earlier lane `5b7dbbab` (not merged), rebuilt here. A wider clear (an item queued by anyone; every sender's run to the member; the member's run to the butler) was built on this lane and removed before it was ready, because the person's words do not ask for it.

## Bounds

With no input from the person, a pair stops at the ninth message. Two things let the butler's run to a member M start again: an item it queued is done, and M's own message to it was delivered, at most eight of those. With k such items the butler has at most 8(k+1) messages to M without answers and 8(k+9) with them. Without an item that is 72, in place of 12 at the limit of three. M to the butler, any other sender to M, and every other direction stay at eight. Each is a minute apart. `session-wake-loop-bound.test.cjs` checks these against the real clear, keeps every earlier case with the number changed, and holds the number in the test itself.

## Checks

Commands, counts and outputs are in the private record of the lane. No model was called, no window was opened, and the daily app was not touched.

## Known limits

- The number of items is not limited by program: each item the butler queues allows it up to eight more messages to that member.
- If NAW stops between recording the item and the call, or the call fails, the run stays until the next item, the person or the member's message clears it. A call that timed out may still clear it later.
- "Once an item" rests on the recorded status in the main process; the backend receives no item id.
- The refusal still says that a person must check the original session, though an item done or an answer also clears the butler's run.
- Not checked in the daily app.

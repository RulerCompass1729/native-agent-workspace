## The size a context handoff starts on is read again first (lane/claude-context-recheck-20261125)

A large warm Claude member is handed over before its next item by a size taken at most every 30 seconds. A member that compacted after the sample, finished, settled (8 seconds) and had its next item within those 30 seconds was handed over by the size from before the compaction, or by a cache lifetime from before a request that missed the cache. It was recorded as a limit in `docs/review/2026-11-24/claude-context-read-20261124.md`.

Now, when a pass would start a handoff for size, the record of that one member is read on to its end and the pass is planned again by what it is. If the member no longer qualifies, it takes its item in the same pass. If another member is then due for its size, its record is read again too, each member at most once a pass. The record is read by the same code as the sample, from where it stopped, up to 48 MB: not read to its end (a line still being written, more than one read takes, a line over 32 MB, gone or unreadable) means no size and no handoff for this reason, and the item is done. Compactions found are counted too, so a third one starts the handoff for that. The other reasons, the sessions not chosen and the sampling interval are as before. From 30 seconds on the sample itself is new and the result is the one it was.

Planning starts over each time with the pass's own lists (waits, pending quiet handoffs, stalls) emptied, so the page does not say a member is being handed over when it was not.

Tests: ten new ones, three of them for the counter alone; nine fail on the earlier code (64ed88e7) and the tenth, 30 seconds after the sample, passes on both. One goes through the whole journey with a real record and counter: sampled at 190,000 while it works, compacted, finished, quiet for 8 seconds, next item 22 seconds after the sample. 16 edits that break these rules: 15 fail at least one test; the other is a line moved unchanged from the sample (an unreadable record's state is dropped), which changes what is read again but no result. The earlier tests' stand-in counters gained a `recheck` that does nothing, and a handoff for compactions is checked not to read again.

Limits: a pass that starts a size handoff opens one file again, and once more on each pass while that member cannot yet be asked (the person is typing, for example); with nothing new that is one open and no bytes. A member not chosen still has a size up to 30 seconds old, which only matters in the direction of a handoff that is missed until the next pass, never one that should not start. Codex has no size, as before.

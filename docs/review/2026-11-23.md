## Session rotation: open decisions go with the successor

Report (the person, 2026-11-23): a queue member with an unanswered decision was never replaced, so long sessions kept growing and cost tokens; asked whether unanswered decisions should go to the successor and the answer follow the rotation chain, the person said yes. Before this batch `rotationReason` returned null for any session named by an open item, a handoff carried only the old session's own text, and an answer on the card went only to the session that asked.

Change (`5b9c7007..b3a1caf5`, lane `claude-rotation-unblock-20261123`):
- `e4ad7144`: the planner's own waiting kinds no longer keep a session.
- `0d38d320`: an open decision no longer keeps its session. The successor's first handoff lists every open decision whole (item id, item text, the member's question) after the old session's text, and only that text is cut when the message is long. Once the successor confirms the handoff, `queueCarry` names the successor on the item and keeps `askedBy` and `carriedFrom`; `queueResolve` takes the recipient, so an answer already on its way to the old session closes the item once there. The card's recipient row follows the item.
- `f401bd77`: when only the cut 200-character line of a question is left, the handoff says so and a second successor keeps the mark. A session whose decision sits in a space it has left stays, as does one whose decisions together pass 10,000 characters.
- `d1d048a3`: while a space is being updated, the move is retried on later passes and the old session is not ended first, within the existing 30-minute stop wait.
- `CLAUDE.md` states the new rule in place of "NAW does not replace that session until the person handles the item".

Review: one independent reviewer session, three rounds over the combined diff (layer one had no earlier independent review, so the whole batch was read). Round 1: BLOCK 1 and notes N1 to N7. The block: the task's acceptance listed "replies and READY after the answer settle on the decision", the change did not build it, and the author's report settled that by a reading. Round 2 (`5b9c7007..f401bd77`): PASS-WITH-NOTES; N3 and N5 fixed; a new note D3; and a delivery condition: the open point is a conflict with a requirement, so it is a hard gate and may not default after five minutes as the author had let it. Round 3 (`5b9c7007..b3a1caf5`): PASS-WITH-NOTES, no blocking finding; D3 fixed; the condition met by a corrected board entry and row V23 of 「与愿景的张力」 in `docs/product.md`.

Not built, waiting for the person (V23): after the person answers a decision, the session's later reply and READY line are kept on no item. `queueResolve` closes the decision when the answer is delivered and the planner settles only sent items; this is the same for a decision that never moved. Building it is a settlement feature outside the task's boundary of rotation timing and answer routing.

Declared limits: two rotations before the first answer receipt refuse that receipt and the item stays waiting (N1); decisions move before the check that may keep the old session (N2); a handoff over the send limit fails before anything moves, and that member is not rotated for seven days (N4); a successor may hold a note for a decision answered meanwhile (N7); a space updated for more than 30 minutes leaves its decision named for the ended session.

Red before green for each step: the layer-two tests failed on `e4ad7144`; three of four named tests failed on `0d38d320` and the control passed; the D3 test failed on `f401bd77` with "the old session stays while its decision has not moved". The 127 related test files at the D3 change: 3444 pass, 0 fail, 0 skipped. No model was called by a test. Assertions changed to the new rule: "a reply that asks for a decision is left for the person" now expects the rotation; the decision test renamed "... and it goes with the session"; one assertion in "an old, quiet space member is rotated even with no work queued"; the layer-one test "a decision the member asked still keeps its session" replaced by three tests; five `queueResolve` expectations in `queue-answer.test.cjs` carry the recipient. ## Answered decisions: the reply after the answer goes on the decision

Report (the person, 2026-11-23): after the person answered a decision on its card, what the session then wrote, including its `READY <branch> <commit>` line, was kept on no item. Asked whether that should change, the person said yes, because otherwise it costs efficiency and tokens. The record "Session rotation: open decisions go with the successor" above left this unbuilt as V23. The butler decided on 2026-11-24 (UTC) that it is built as a separate item; the row is now V24, since another lane uses V23.

Change (`edf98ef3..f261b20d`, lane `claude-rotation-unblock-20261123`):
- `37440ed2`: `queueResolve` still records the item done when the answer is delivered, and keeps the message row the answer was delivered as (`answerTag`) when the item's current session received it. Once that session is idle and settled, the planner reads what it wrote after that row, up to the next input that starts other work; `queueAnswered` adds the reply and the READY line to the item and removes the mark. The READY fact is recorded once per item. The desktop card passes the row id with `queueResolve`; the phone path keeps it in the broker and does not send it to the phone. A decision carried to a successor is read from the successor.
- `56a40d2b`: an answer read inside a turn that was already running adds nothing.
- `f261b20d`: row V24 names the cases that add nothing.

Review: the independent reviewer session that reviewed the rotation batch, two rounds. Round 1 (`edf98ef3..37440ed2`): BLOCK 1. A member on its next item reads the card answer inside that turn (the row carries `deliveredInto`), and the turn's last reply, with that item's READY line, was recorded on the decision, with a READY fact under the decision's key. Reproduced as a test: it failed on `37440ed2` (316 tests in the file, 1 failure, the new test) and passes with `56a40d2b`. Round 2 (`37440ed2..f261b20d`): PASS-WITH-NOTES for `edf98ef3..f261b20d`; the finding is closed and no new wrong outcome was found.

Cases that add nothing to the decision (the mark is removed and the item stays as the person left it): the answer was read inside a running turn; the earlier session received the answer after a rotation; the session is gone or archived; the row is not the person's message; 24 hours passed without a reply. An unanswered decision is never settled by this path.

Declared limits: the reply is read only while the idle clock runs (automatic messages on and not paused). A row the session record never holds (older readers rename a queued row) keeps local passes going for up to 24 hours, with no model call; clearing as soon as the session rests was rejected, because a pass can see the mark before its session snapshot holds the row. An item whose question already carried a READY line keeps that line and its one READY fact, while its reply text changes to the later reply; one READY fact per item is fixed by key in `app/butler-ready.cjs`, `app/butler-business.cjs` and `app/integrator-engine.cjs`, and changing that is left as a separate decision. A later reply that asks a new decision is recorded as text and does not reopen the card. The person's later messages in the same session are not a boundary. Whether a stored mid-turn message can run as its own turn after its turn ends was not verified; such an answer also adds nothing.

## Session rotation: open decisions go with the successor

Report (the person, 2026-11-23): a queue member with an unanswered decision was never replaced, so long sessions kept growing and cost tokens; asked whether unanswered decisions should go to the successor and the answer follow the rotation chain, the person said yes. Before this batch `rotationReason` returned null for any session named by an open item, a handoff carried only the old session's own text, and an answer on the card went only to the session that asked.

Change (`5b9c7007..b3a1caf5`, lane `claude-rotation-unblock-20261123`):
- `e4ad7144`: the planner's own waiting kinds no longer keep a session.
- `0d38d320`: an open decision no longer keeps its session. The successor's first handoff lists every open decision whole (item id, item text, the member's question) after the old session's text, and only that text is cut when the message is long. Once the successor confirms the handoff, `queueCarry` names the successor on the item and keeps `askedBy` and `carriedFrom`; `queueResolve` takes the recipient, so an answer already on its way to the old session closes the item once there. The card's recipient row follows the item.
- `f401bd77`: when only the cut 200-character line of a question is left, the handoff says so and a second successor keeps the mark. A session whose decision sits in a space it has left stays, as does one whose decisions together pass 10,000 characters.
- `d1d048a3`: while a space is being updated, the move is retried on later passes and the old session is not ended first, within the existing 30-minute stop wait.
- `CLAUDE.md` states the new rule in place of "NAW does not replace that session until the person handles the item".

Not built, waiting for the person (V23): after the person answers a decision, the session's later reply and READY line are kept on no item. `queueResolve` closes the decision when the answer is delivered and the planner settles only sent items; this is the same for a decision that never moved. Building it is a settlement feature outside the task's boundary of rotation timing and answer routing.

Declared limits: two rotations before the first answer receipt refuse that receipt and the item stays waiting (N1); decisions move before the check that may keep the old session (N2); a handoff over the send limit fails before anything moves, and that member is not rotated for seven days (N4); a successor may hold a note for a decision answered meanwhile (N7); a space updated for more than 30 minutes leaves its decision named for the ended session.

## Butler not woken: orphaned deferral, refused messages, the person's step on the banner (R129)

Report: the person, 11-23: 「"没被叫醒"是个 bug,需要修」. The butler's wakes were held as rotation-control-unresolved from 11-19 through every restart.

Finding: code before alpha.284 deferred the butler's rotation for the person (rotation log: requested, then waiting person-active), dropped its record and left the backend episode invalid. Nothing NAW ran afterwards ended it.

Fix (5847b1f2, d178dbec):
- On a backend at alpha.284 or later the queue dispatcher ends exactly that logged deferral through the existing person-active invalidation. It acts as NAW, once per backend version, and the session then rests as a deferral does. Any other invalid episode stays the person's. An older backend's hold names updating it first.
- A workspace_message to the butler refused because a compacted turn has no root binding is now recorded in its events. Together with the two reasons R119 records, all three not-sent reasons are covered, and a replaced butler's records go under the current one.
- The wake banner offers the person's step as the same call as the sidebar item, and counts the messages recorded as not delivered. The phone says to take that step on the computer; its push is unchanged.

- Related sets: 6089 tests and then 1686 tests. Three failures, all of which also fail on clean main bbbd56fe (DSH privacy patch resolution, integrator folder choice, a Monitor notification fixture).

Independent review:

## Butler wakes for refusals: past one record page, and after a release (288, follows the 287 review)

Source: two non-blocking items from the 287 review, which the project manager sent for repair.
- (1) The check for refusals read one page of the record, at most 100 events or about 8 KiB. A longer cycle counted as news, so more than 100 refusals between wakes woke the butler every minute with no limit.
- (2) Only a call of the butler's that went through reset the count. If the person released the butler from the card, refusals after that did not wake it.

Fix (0b3f6f14):
- The check now reads up to 20 pages. Any other event in those pages makes the wake ordinary news. Refusals past the pages read still count as refusals only, so news recorded after more than 20 pages of refusals waits while the butler is at the limit. Its banner shows at once.
- A release the dispatcher confirms now resets the count. That release is either the person's from a window or the butler's own. A refused, outdated-backend or unconfirmed release resets nothing.
- Unchanged: the limit of 3, the 60 s interval and every refusal condition.

Tests (no model calls):
- Three new tests fail on the parent 7dc97609 and pass on the fix (13/13). Covered: a 101-event cycle (4 sends before the fix, 3 and then the limit after), a bounded-read check, and a reset after a release.
- Related sets: 67 files, 1816 tests, with 2 failures. Both also fail on clean main (integrator folder choice, a Monitor notification fixture).

Independent review: headless Opus with read-only tools. Pass, with four non-blocking notes and no change needed:
- A ledger page that is empty but reports more events. The real ledger cannot return one.
- The bounded-read fake never advances.
- The worst-case read size, the same as an existing view.
- A release of any session resets the count. This is the stated choice.
